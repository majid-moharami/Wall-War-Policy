3. Device or Other Identifiers (Play Console Declaration)

In our Play Console Data Safety declaration, we explicitly disclose the collection and processing of Device or other IDs:

Technical Breakdown of Collected Identifiers
  • Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) / UUID: When playing as Guest, the app reads a hardware-backed identifier or generates a unique UUID. Transmitted securely via TLS to our Nakama server, this allows guest duelists to retain their coins, skins, and trophies across app restarts without forcing account registration, while preventing multi-account smurfing and matchmaking abuse.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Adivery SDK for optional rewarded video ads (e.g. watch a video to receive bonus in-game coins). Players can reset or delete their advertising ID at any time via: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Firebase Installation ID (FID) & FCM Token: Assigned to uniquely route match-turn push notifications and measure installation retention metrics.
  • Nakama Server User ID: A unique cryptographic UUID generated on our multiplayer servers to match duelists into competitive arenas and update seasonal leaderboard rankings.

4. Location Data Minimization (Google Play Compliance)

Addressing Google Play's "Minimize your use of location data to protect user privacy" policy:

Zero Geolocation Tracking Guarantee
  • Zero Location Permissions: WallWar does not declare or request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, or background location permissions in its manifest.
  • No GPS or Sensor Tracking: We do not track, collect, record, or share GPS coordinates or geographic paths of users.
  • Network Routing: Standard IP addresses are used solely at the socket layer to route multiplayer packets to the nearest game server and are not converted into precise physical user addresses.

5. Personal Information Sharing & No Sale of Data

Addressing Google Play's "Shares Personal Information" policy and international privacy mandates:

🚫 100% No Sale of Data

WallWar does never sell, rent, lease, or monetize user personal data, device identifiers, or gameplay records to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

Data is only transmitted to certified technical infrastructure providers (Google Play Services, Firebase, Nakama, AdMob, Adivery) exclusively to execute necessary game functions.

6. Purpose of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  1. Core App Functionality: Real-time player-versus-player (PvP) matchmaking, validating wall placements and pawn moves, recording match history, maintaining leaderboards, and honoring virtual coin balances.
  2. Account Authentication & State Synchronization: Allowing players to save duel records locally and in the cloud, and restore progress across sessions.
  3. Security, Anti-Cheat & Fraud Prevention: Preventing automated botting, detecting modified game clients, verifying in-app billing tokens, and maintaining fair competitive integrity.
  4. Analytics & Diagnostics: Monitoring server uptime, identifying disconnect causes during live online matches, and assessing which arenas or skins are popular.
  5. Advertising & In-Game Monetization: Serving optional rewarded video ads and interstitials through verified mobile ad networks.

8. Data Security & Encryption in Transit

We are committed to state-of-the-art data protection standards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network traffic between WallWar and backend servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3, HTTPS) and Secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • Payment Card Safety: WallWar never handles or stores payment card data. All financial transactions are executed exclusively within Google Play's PCI-DSS compliant billing infrastructure.
  • Access Controls: Server administrative interfaces and game databases are strictly secured behind firewall barriers and tokenized authentication.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy regarding account and data deletion, players have complete control over their personal data:

1

Choose In-App Sign Out or Email Deletion

You can disconnect your Google account inside the game under Profile > Sign Out or request total profile erasure via email.

2

Send Deletion Request

Email either wallwar.game@gmail.com or Majid.moharami79@gmail.com with subject "WallWar - Data Deletion Request" including your player display name or Google Play email.

3

Permanent Purge Within 30 Days

All Nakama user records, trophy rankings, and match histories are permanently purged from all production servers within 30 calendar days.
