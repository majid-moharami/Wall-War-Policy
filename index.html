3. Device or Other Identifiers (Play Console Declaration)

In our Play Console Data Safety declaration, we explicitly disclose the collection of Device or other IDs. Here is exactly how and why they are utilized:

  • Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) / UUID: When a player chooses "Play as Guest", the app reads the device's hardware-backed identifier or generates a unique UUID. This identifier is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our dedicated game server (Heroic Labs Nakama) to instantiate a persistent guest profile. This allows guest players to retain their earned virtual coins, board skins, and trophy rankings across app restarts without forcing them to register an email address. It also serves as a critical defense against smurfing and matchmaking abuse.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Adivery SDK to deliver non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads (allowing players to watch an optional video ad in exchange for in-game coins). Players can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through Android System Settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Firebase Installation ID (FID) & FCM Token: Assigned uniquely to the app installation instance to facilitate push notifications (such as daily reward reminders or opponent turn notifications) and measure overall installation retention metrics.
  • Nakama Server User ID: A unique cryptographic UUID generated on our multiplayer servers to match duelists into competitive arenas and update seasonal leaderboard rankings.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate and disclosed operational purposes:

  1. Core App Functionality: Real-time player-versus-player (PvP) matchmaking, validating wall placements and pawn moves, recording match history, maintaining leaderboards, and honoring virtual coin balances.
  2. Account Authentication & State Synchronization: Allowing players to save duel records locally and in the cloud, and restore progress across sessions.
  3. Security, Anti-Cheat & Fraud Prevention: Preventing automated botting, detecting modified game clients, verifying in-app billing tokens, and maintaining fair competitive integrity.
  4. Analytics & Diagnostics: Monitoring server uptime, identifying disconnect causes during live online matches, and assessing which arenas or skins are popular.
  5. Advertising & In-Game Monetization: Serving optional rewarded video ads and interstitials through verified mobile ad networks.

6. Data Security & Encryption in Transit

We are committed to protecting user data through state-of-the-art security practices:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the WallWar client application and backend servers (including Nakama APIs, Firebase, and Google APIs) is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3, HTTPS) and Secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • Payment Card Safety: WallWar never handles or stores sensitive payment card details or banking data. All payment transactions are executed exclusively within Google Play's PCI-DSS compliant billing infrastructure.
  • Access Controls: Server administrative interfaces and game databases are strictly secured behind firewall barriers and tokenized authentication.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy regarding account and data deletion, users have full control over their personal data and game accounts.

How Long We Retain Data

Account profiles, match histories, and trophy scores are retained as long as your account remains active. If an account is inactive for more than 24 consecutive months or if a deletion request is received, the associated profile and identifiers are permanently purged or anonymized.

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via either of the following methods:
