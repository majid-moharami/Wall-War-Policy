3. Device or Other Identifiers (Play Console Declaration)

In our Play Console Data Safety declaration, we explicitly disclose the collection of Device or other IDs:

  • Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) / UUID: When playing as Guest, the app uses a hardware-backed identifier or unique UUID transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS to our Nakama multiplayer server. This enables players to preserve earned coins, unlocked board skins, and trophy rankings across app restarts without forcing account registration, while preventing multi-account smurfing and matchmaking abuse.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Utilized by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Adivery SDK for optional rewarded video ads (e.g., watch an ad to earn in-game coins) and interstitial ads. Users can reset or delete their advertising ID at any time in Android Settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID).
  • Firebase Installation ID (FID) & FCM Token: Assigned uniquely to the app installation instance to facilitate push notifications (such as daily reward reminders or opponent turn notifications) and measure overall installation retention metrics.
  • Nakama Server User ID: A unique cryptographic UUID generated on our multiplayer servers to match duelists into competitive arenas and update seasonal leaderboard rankings.

4. Location Data Minimization (Google Play Policy Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play's "Minimize your use of location data to protect user privacy" guidance:

  • Zero Location Permissions: WallWar does not declare or request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, or background location permissions in its manifest.
  • No GPS or Geolocation Tracking: We do not track, collect, record, or share GPS coordinates or geographic paths of users.
  • Network Routing: Standard IP addresses are used solely at the socket layer to route multiplayer packets to the nearest game server and are not converted into precise physical user addresses.

5. Personal Information Sharing & No Sale of Data

In accordance with Google Play's "Shares Personal Information" policy and global data protection standards:

  • No Sale of Data: WallWar does never sell, rent, lease, or monetize user personal data, device identifiers, or gameplay records to third-party data brokers or advertisers.
  • Strict Service Provider Sharing: Data is only transmitted to certified technical infrastructure providers (Google Play Services, Firebase, Nakama, AdMob, Adivery) exclusively to execute necessary game functions (matchmaking, leaderboards, crash monitoring, and ad serving).
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not merge user identity across unrelated applications or track users across external third-party apps or websites.

6. Purpose of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for disclosed operational purposes:

  1. Core App Functionality: Real-time player-versus-player (PvP) matchmaking, validating wall placements and pawn moves, recording match history, maintaining leaderboards, and honoring virtual coin balances.
  2. Account Authentication & State Synchronization: Allowing players to save duel records locally and in the cloud, and restore progress across sessions.
  3. Security, Anti-Cheat & Fraud Prevention: Preventing automated botting, detecting modified game clients, verifying in-app billing tokens, and maintaining fair competitive integrity.
  4. Analytics & Diagnostics: Monitoring server uptime, identifying disconnect causes during live online matches, and assessing which arenas or skins are popular.
  5. Advertising & In-Game Monetization: Serving optional rewarded video ads and interstitials through verified mobile ad networks.

8. Data Security & Encryption in Transit

We are committed to protecting user data through state-of-the-art security practices:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the WallWar client application and backend servers (including Nakama APIs, Firebase, and Google APIs) is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3, HTTPS) and Secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • Payment Card Safety: WallWar never handles or stores sensitive payment card details or banking data. All payment transactions are executed exclusively within Google Play's PCI-DSS compliant billing infrastructure.
  • Access Controls: Server administrative interfaces and game databases are strictly secured behind firewall barriers and tokenized authentication.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy regarding account and data deletion, users have full control over their personal data and game accounts.

How Long We Retain Data

Account profiles, match histories, and trophy scores are retained as long as your account remains active. If an account is inactive for more than 24 consecutive months or if a deletion request is received, the associated profile and identifiers are permanently purged or anonymized.

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via either of the following methods:

  1. Via Email (Recommended): Send an email to wallwar.game@gmail.com or Majid.moharami79@gmail.com with the subject line "WallWar - Data Deletion Request". Please include your in-game Display Name, user ID (from the in-game Profile or Settings screen), or the Google Account email associated with the game.
  2. In-App Sign Out: You can disconnect your Google account from the game at any time by navigating to Profile > Sign Out.

Processing Timeline: Once a verified deletion request is received, all associated user records—including your Nakama account, custom uploaded avatar, leaderboard records, and match history—will be permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.
